Fence services

Fence options built around your property and priorities

Different fence types solve different problems. This page gives property owners a straightforward way to compare common options before requesting an estimate.

Wood Fencing

Wood fencing is often chosen when a property owner wants warmth, privacy, and flexibility in style. It can work well for backyard boundaries, side-yard separation, and visible fence lines where appearance matters.

  • Useful for privacy, character, and custom layouts
  • Common for backyard enclosures and decorative street-facing runs
  • Can be adapted to traditional, horizontal, or custom looks
  • Worth considering if you are comfortable with periodic upkeep

Vinyl Fencing

Vinyl fencing is typically considered by homeowners who want a clean, consistent look with lower routine maintenance. It can make sense for privacy fencing, side-yard boundaries, and family yards.

  • Useful when easy upkeep is a priority
  • Common for homes that want privacy with a polished finish
  • Available in styles that can complement different home exteriors
  • Important to compare appearance, layout, and site conditions first

Chain-Link Fencing

Chain-link fencing is a practical option for security, pets, property boundaries, and utility areas. It is often chosen when function, visibility, and straightforward enclosure matter most.

  • Useful for security and defined boundaries
  • Common around side yards, rear yards, dog runs, and utility zones
  • Can be a practical choice when visibility is preferred over full privacy
  • Worth reviewing based on appearance goals and neighborhood context

Privacy Fencing

Privacy fencing is about reducing visibility and creating a more comfortable outdoor area. It is often used where yards are close together, outdoor living space is important, or screening is part of the goal.

  • Useful for private backyards and outdoor gathering space
  • Common where neighboring sight lines feel too open
  • Can support both comfort and a more finished property layout
  • Important to consider height, style, and overall yard use

Fence Replacement

Fence replacement is often the right next step when an existing fence is aging, damaged, leaning, or no longer fits how the property is being used. Replacement projects also create an opportunity to improve layout and access.

  • Useful when repair is no longer the best long-term choice
  • Common after storm damage, wear, or property updates
  • Allows property owners to revisit material and design choices
  • Helpful to think through removal, new layout, and gate needs together

Gates

Gates help the fence work in daily life. A good gate solution should feel intentional, fit the fence style, and support how people, pets, equipment, or vehicles move through the property.

  • Useful for access, convenience, and controlled entry
  • Common for walk gates, side-yard gates, and wider driveway openings
  • Can be planned as part of a new fence or a replacement project
  • Worth considering based on clearance, use frequency, and location
